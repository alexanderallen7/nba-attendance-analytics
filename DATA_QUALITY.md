The source database has no attendance value for roughly 8-10% of games
in every season; this is a gap in the source's `game_info` table, not
concentrated in any team or month. Those rows are kept in the extract
with a null fill rate and drop out of the regression and fill-rate views.

The source is also missing the 2012-13 season entirely, which is one
reason the window starts at 2013-14.

## Attendance above capacity (cap rule)

- Non-excluded games with reported attendance above listed capacity: **2,345**
  (max ratio 1.133).

Teams routinely report attendance above listed seating capacity (standing
room, suites, temporary seating). Rule chosen and applied everywhere:
`fill_rate` = attendance / capacity **capped at 1.0**; the uncapped value is
kept in `fill_rate_raw`; `sellout_flag` = attendance >= capacity.

## Known residual issues

Two Toronto home games in December 2021 were played under Ontario's
50% COVID capacity restriction and are kept (not excluded), since the
league-wide 2021-22 season was otherwise at full capacity:

- 2021-12-18 TOR: attendance 7,988
- 2021-12-28 TOR: attendance 6,960

- Other non-excluded games with attendance below 6,000: **0**.
- Attendance is team-reported (tickets distributed, not turnstile count);
  several arenas report identical sellout figures for long stretches.
- Capacity for stable arenas is the current figure from Wikipedia's arena
  list; minor mid-era reconfigurations below ~500 seats are not modeled.

## Leakage guard

- Rows where a team was playing its first game of the season carry a null
  entering win percentage rather than a leaked value: **163** rows affected.
- `tests/test_no_leakage.py` recomputes every entering record independently
  and fails if any entering value includes the current game or later games.
